# Separate engineering qualification from attachment governance and report rendering

The private case bundle records unsigned, self-attested internal evidence: a real or synthetic case, immutable calculation runs, per-run independent comparisons, discrepancy dispositions, reviewed artifacts, and explicit adoption receipts. Each comparison assertion extracts its value through a JSON Pointer from separate production-result and independent-reference JSON artifacts; the normalized comparison binds both machine-readable artifacts, the human reference, and the production output by SHA-256. Reviewed and rendered artifacts must visibly retain the same fixed 16-hex calculation fingerprint. A PDF additionally binds its sibling canonical-render evidence by relative path, bytes, and SHA-256, and is accepted only when the attachment inspector reports verified visibility rather than text-layer-only extraction. A synthetic case or one to two pilot cases may contribute G1 evidence but cannot establish a tool-wide verification domain; G2 and G3 require a real case and remain limited to the same run and artifact. Existing formal-attachment governance continues to prove document identity, approval metadata, packaging integrity, and archive lineage; it must not infer engineering equivalence from `ready`. A report renderer such as docxtpl may consume only same-run G3-adopted artifact data without recalculating results or copying approval. Internal G1/G2/G3 records do not authenticate the reviewer, constitute certification, or provide legal sign-off. This intermediate boundary costs one additional contract and review step, but prevents per-page browser drafts, package integrity, synthetic fixtures, pilot cases, or editable Word output from becoming an accidental source of engineering truth.
